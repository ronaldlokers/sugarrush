## Design specificity

Authored, not adapted — cards name their window, forecasts vanish in a sensor gap, hover snaps to real readings, days group at local midnight, treatments sit off the line. Exception: the Settings view, whose SettingRow/SwitchRow are generic preference rows.

Detector: not applicable. `.qml` is absent from SCANNABLE_EXTENSIONS, so detect.mjs scanned 0 of 9 files, printed `[]`, exited 0, with no "unsupported filetype" signal. No browser/overlay: Quickshell renders via Qt scene graph on Wayland. Panel could not be surfaced via IPC on either monitor for fresh captures; screenshots used predate the two-column layout.

## Priority issues

- **[P0] Chart never receives its settings.** Panel.qml:1610 passes only doc/foreground; Chart.qml defaults viewHours:6, scrollbackHours:72. "Chart window" relabels the card without changing the chart; "Scroll back" does nothing. Chart.windowLabel and Chart.follow() are referenced nowhere in Panel.qml. Fix: bind viewHours/scrollbackHours, bind label to chart.windowLabel when panned, tap-to-follow. → /impeccable harden
- **[P0] Alarms card delegates render off-panel in wide mode.** Card is halfOf(column.width) ≈384px; both Repeater delegates use `column.width - Style.space(24)` (full Flow width). Timestamps land ~390px past the card edge inside a clipped Flickable; same on the failed-delivery repeater. Fix: parent.width + elide. → /impeccable adapt
- **[P1] The pill never says the watcher is dead.** "not watching" renders only in a strip gated to Glucose inside a click-to-open panel; BarWidget has no concept of watcher_alive. → /impeccable harden
- **[P1] Last night is the fourth card** (nowCard → statsCard → chart → nightCard), contradicting the README's "the Glucose view leads with the night just gone". → /impeccable layout
- **[P1] Any press on the threshold band commits a write.** nearest() has no distance cutoff; no undo; 3px handles; "9px monospace" labels off-theme. Fix: 12px grab radius + inline undo. → /impeccable harden

## Persona red flags

- Hyprland power user: navigation is keyboard-reachable, no action is. No `s` for snooze. Eight `focusable: false` overrides, no focus ring.
- Newly diagnosed: GMI/CV/Δ unglossed, five-band bar with no legend, `low 10.6` on a high night, "urgent low" as a draggable handle label.
- Colourblind: betterColor() reuses the glucose ladder for "better/worse"; the QML fallback palette collapses low/high and both urgents, making DayStrip position-only.
